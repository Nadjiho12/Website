Who am I? 01

I'm Nadji, a cybersecurity student who learns by doing. Most of my time goes into hands-on labs on Hack The Box and TryHackMe, where I practice the offensive side: reconnaissance, web application testing and Active Directory attack paths. I balance that with the defensive side: log analysis, threat detection and incident response.

I started with the fundamentals of networking, Windows and Linux, and built up from there toward penetration testing. Alongside security, I'm learning AI properly, from the fundamentals up, because I build security tools with it.

What I care about most is building tools that respect privacy: they run on your own machine, keep sensitive data local and leave a person in charge of every decision. NASIS is the project where I put that into practice.

I'm a native Arabic speaker and I work in English.
